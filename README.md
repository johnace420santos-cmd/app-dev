 # Moon Knight
**Platform:** *Disney*
**Release Date:** *March 30, 2022*
**Episodes:** *6 (Limited Series)*
**Genre:** *Action, Psychological Thriller, Superhero*
**Based on:** *Marvel Comics character Moon Knight (created by Doug Moench and Don Perlin)*

#Main Story / Plot
*The series follows Marc Spector, a former mercenary suffering from dissociative identity disorder (DID). He becomes the human avatar of Khonshu, the Egyptian moon god of vengeance. Marc’s different identities — including Steven Grant, a mild-mannered gift shop employee, and Jake Lockley, a mysterious third personality — all struggle for control as he uncovers dark secrets about his past and destiny. As the story unfolds, Marc and Steven must stop Arthur Harrow, a cult leader who wants to unleash the goddess Ammit, who punishes people for crimes before they’re committed.*
